Three ways in

For musicians

Perform at a senior home

Any instrument, any voice, any skill level you're comfortable playing in front of people. We add you to the roster and check in before each show to see who can make it, so signing up doesn't lock you into a date.

Volunteer hour certificates at every 5 hours. Performances and build meetings both count toward the total.

Check our Performances tab for upcoming dates.

For makers

Build devices for people with disabilities

Help us 3D print and assemble assistive devices and adapted toys through Makers Making Change, or tell us about someone who could use one. You don't need a printer of your own, build meetings need hands more than they need hardware.

Not sure what this involves? The Build page walks through how a build meeting runs.

Volunteer hour certificates at every 5 hours. Performances and build meetings both count toward the total.
Everything else

Ask about something else

Marketing, outreach, design, donations, media, partnerships, or something we haven't thought of yet. If you run a care home and want music in your building, this is the form to use.
