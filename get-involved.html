Volunteer hours

Every 4 hours of performing or building earns a volunteer hour certificate. Both programs count toward the same total, and travel to and from a show counts as part of the day.

  • Hours are logged on the day by an organiser.
  • Certificates are issued as PDFs, signed and dated.
  • Nothing expires — hours accumulate across the year.

Volunteer Service Letter Request

You can request a Volunteer Service Letter at any performance — ask an organiser on the day and we’ll take your details there. Or email us and we’ll write it the same week.

  • Addressed to a school, scholarship, or award committee.
  • States your role, dates played, and total hours.
  • Sent as a signed PDF, or printed on request.
