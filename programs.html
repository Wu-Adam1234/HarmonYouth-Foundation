Programs

Two programs, one reason

Students showing up for people who don’t always get thought of first — once with instruments, once with a soldering iron and a box of 3D-printed parts.

Program 01 Music

Live music at senior homes

Five to ten student musicians perform a 30 to 45 minute set in the home’s common room — mostly solo pieces with a few group numbers to close. We play the same homes month after month, so residents recognise faces and staff can put us on the calendar in advance.

  • Piano, strings, voice, clarinet, cello — whatever you bring.
  • No audition. Any level you’re comfortable playing in front of people.
  • We check in before every date; you say yes to the ones that work.
  • Volunteer hours logged on the day, certificate every 4 hours.

Program 02 Build

Assistive devices and adapted toys

Members 3D print and assemble devices from the Makers Making Change open-source library, a program of the Neil Squire Society. The designs are reviewed by occupational therapists; finished devices go to people in our community at little to no cost.

  • You don’t need a printer — parts are printed ahead of the meeting.
  • Build nights need hands: assembly, wiring switches, testing.
  • Adapted toys let kids with limited motor control play independently.
  • Build hours count toward the same volunteer total as performing.
