Our mission

Two facts, put in the same room

HarmonYouth Foundation exists because two things were true at once in Calgary: students had music they’d practised for years with nowhere meaningful to play it, and senior homes had residents who went weeks without anything on the calendar. We put those two facts in the same room, every month.

01Consistency

Regular beats spectacular

A single impressive concert is a nice afternoon. Two shows a month, at the same homes, becomes something residents plan around — and something staff can promise.

02Access

No auditions, no fees

Any instrument, any level you’re comfortable playing in front of people. Nobody pays to join and nobody is turned away for not being conservatory-ready.

03Transparency

We keep none of the money

All proceeds and donations go to the hospitals and senior homes we support. Students volunteer their time; nothing is deducted for us.
