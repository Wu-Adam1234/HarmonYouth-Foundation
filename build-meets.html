What to expect

Three hours, a table full of parts, and a finished device at the end

We print the pieces ahead of time. At the meet, we sort them, put them together, wire up any switches, and test that everything actually works before it goes to the person who asked for it. Bring nothing but yourself, though a phone for the assembly instructions helps.

Volunteer hour certificates at every 4 hours. Performances and build meetings both count toward the total, and we can write a volunteer service letter on request.
