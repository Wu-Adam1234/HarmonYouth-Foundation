Build meets

Three hours, a table full of parts, a finished device

Build meets are where we assemble the 3D printed assistive devices and adapted toys we make through Makers Making Change. No printer and no experience needed — we’ll show you what to do when you get there.

What to expect

Bring nothing but yourself

We print the pieces ahead of time. At the meet, we sort them, put them together, wire up any switches, and test that everything actually works before it goes to the person who asked for it. A phone for the assembly instructions helps.

01

We print ahead

Parts come off the printer before the meeting, so nobody waits on a machine.

02

Sort and assemble

Pieces get sorted, then put together following the Makers Making Change instructions.

03

Wire and test

Switches get wired up and everything is tested before it leaves the table.

04

Hand it on

The finished device goes to the person who asked for it, at little to no cost.
