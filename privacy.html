Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we don’t

HarmonYouth Foundation is a student-run organisation in Calgary, Alberta. We keep as little personal information as we can, and we never sell it. This page explains the rest in plain language.

Last updated: August 31, 2026Questions? harmonyouthfoundation@gmail.com
01

Information we collect

When you join the roster, book a performance, request a volunteer service letter, or sign up for show reminders, we collect the details you send us — typically your name, email address, instrument, school or organisation, and the dates you are available. We do not run accounts, and there is nothing to log into.

02

Why we collect it

To schedule performances, confirm who is playing on a given date, log volunteer hours, issue certificates and service letters, and reply to you. That is the whole list.

03

Photographs at performances

We take photos at shows and post a small selection to this site and to Instagram. We ask the home and the people in frame first, and we do not caption residents by name. If you appear in a photo and would rather not, email us and we will remove it — no questions asked, usually within a day.

04

Students under 18

Most of our performers are minors. For anyone under 18 we ask a parent or guardian to confirm participation by email, and we only publish a first name if the guardian is comfortable with it. Guardians can withdraw that permission at any time.

05

Email reminders

If you sign up for show reminders, you get one short email before each performance and nothing else. Every email has an unsubscribe line, and unsubscribing removes your address from the list entirely.

06

Third-party services

Donations are handled by GoFundMe, which processes payment information under its own privacy policy — we never see your card details. Our signup forms are delivered by Formspree, which passes the message to our email and stores a copy in our dashboard. Photos are also posted to Instagram, which has its own terms. Our Build program uses designs from Makers Making Change, a program of the Neil Squire Society; no personal data is shared with them.

07

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Your colour-mode preference is stored locally in your own browser and never sent to us. Embedded content, such as the GoFundMe widget, may set its own cookies.

08

How long we keep things

Roster and volunteer-hour records are kept while you are active and for one year after your last performance, so we can still write a service letter if you ask. Booking correspondence with homes is kept for our own scheduling history. Anything else is deleted on request.

09

Your requests

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a correction, ask for a photo to come down, or ask us to delete your details entirely. Email harmonyouthfoundation@gmail.com or call (403) 926-6122 and we will act on it.

10

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of the page. Material changes will also be mentioned in the next reminder email.
