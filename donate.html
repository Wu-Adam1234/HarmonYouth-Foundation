Every dollar keeps this running

Donations go into running the organisation — printing, the volunteer hour certificates we issue, and gifts. HarmonYouth is run entirely by students, so there is no office and no administrative cut. We’re raising $1,000 to keep it running. Donations are processed by Stripe — the button below opens our secure Stripe donation page in a new tab. HarmonYouth never sees or stores your card details.
